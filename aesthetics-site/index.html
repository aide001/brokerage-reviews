How we work

Medicine first, treatment second.

Lots of clinics nearby are doctor-led. What makes the difference is what happens in the room: a proper assessment, a doctor who takes responsibility for your care from the first visit to the last, and advice you can trust when the answer is "this isn't right for you".

A real medical assessment

Your medical history, medicines and goals are reviewed before anything is recommended. Some people are better suited to skincare or no treatment at all.

The same doctor every time

Dr Morris assesses you, plans your care, treats you and reviews the result. No hand-offs between a prescriber and a separate injector.

We'll tell you "not today"

If a treatment won't give you the result you want, or isn't safe for you right now, we'll say so and explain why.

Care that continues

A review after treatment, a direct line for aftercare questions, and a clear plan if anything doesn't settle as expected.

"A careful medical assessment, a plan that still looks like you, and the same doctor there for your follow-up."

Your visit

What happens, in order.

You're never treated on impulse. Most people have their consultation and treatment on different days, so you have time to think it over.

  1. Consultation

    Medical history, examination, photographs with your consent, and an honest talk about what's realistic.

  2. Written plan

    Your options, expected results, risks and exact prices, sent to you to read at home.

  3. Treatment

    When you're ready, Dr Morris treats you and goes through aftercare before you leave.

  4. Review

    A follow-up at about two weeks to check the result, fine-tune if needed and plan any next steps.

Questions

Before you book.

If your question isn't here, send it with the form below and the doctor will reply.

Do I have to have treatment after my consultation?

No. Many people go home with a written plan and decide later, and some decide not to go ahead. The consultation fee is taken off your treatment if you do book.

Will I look "done"?

Our aim is that people notice you look well rested, not that you've had something done. We start conservatively and build at your review if needed.

Why don't you list prices for wrinkle treatments?

Some treatments for lines and wrinkles use prescription-only medicines, which UK law doesn't allow clinics to advertise to the public. A doctor has to assess you in person first, and we'll give you full prices at your consultation.

How long do results last?

It depends on the treatment, the area and your own metabolism. Typical ranges are covered in your written plan so you know what to expect before you commit.

Is there anyone you can't treat?

Yes. We don't treat anyone under 18, or anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding, and some medical conditions and medicines mean treatment isn't suitable. We'll check this at your consultation.

What if I'm not happy, or something goes wrong?

Contact the clinic straight away using the aftercare details you're given. Dr Morris will review you and, if needed, arrange further treatment or referral. Complaints are handled by a written procedure.
