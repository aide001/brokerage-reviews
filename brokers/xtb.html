Our verdict on XTB

XTB was founded in 2002, is listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange and serves UK clients through XTB Limited, which is authorised by the FCA (FRN 522157). It has grown fast and now reports more than 2.9 million clients.

Costs are simple. There is no commission on CFDs (costs are in the spread, from 0.8 pips on major forex pairs), no minimum deposit, and deposits and withdrawals are free. For investors, XTB offers commission-free shares and ETFs up to €100,000 of monthly turnover, plus a Stocks and Shares ISA and a Cash ISA.

The main limitation is platform choice. XTB only offers its own xStation platform and app, with no MetaTrader, TradingView or spread betting. xStation is polished and beginner-friendly, but traders who rely on Expert Advisors will need to look elsewhere. An inactivity fee applies after 12 months without trading (ISAs excluded).

Is XTB safe?

  • XTB Limited is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 522157).
  • Client funds are segregated from XTB's own money.
  • Retail accounts have negative balance protection.
  • Parent company XTB S.A. is listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange and publishes its earnings and cash reserves every quarter.
  • Eligible UK clients may be covered by the FSCS (currently up to £85,000 for investments) if the UK firm fails.

XTB FAQs

Is XTB safe?

XTB Limited is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 522157). Client money is segregated, retail accounts have negative balance protection, and the parent company is listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange.

What is XTB's minimum deposit?

There is no minimum deposit. Deposits by card or bank transfer are free.

Does XTB charge an inactivity fee?

Yes. XTB charges £10 (or the €10 equivalent) a month if you have not traded for 12 months and have not deposited in the last 90 days. ISAs are exempt.

Does XTB offer MT4 or TradingView?

No. XTB only offers its own xStation platform and mobile app.

Does XTB offer an ISA?

Yes. XTB offers a Stocks and Shares ISA and a Cash ISA to UK clients.

How fast are XTB withdrawals?

Withdrawal requests made before 1pm are sent the same business day, and after 1pm the next business day. XTB says funds can take up to 3 business days to arrive.
