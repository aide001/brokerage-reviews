Our verdict on Saxo

Saxo has been in business since 1992 and serves UK clients through Saxo Capital Markets UK Ltd, which is authorised by the FCA (FRN 551422). It is a multi-asset platform first and a forex broker second: from one account you can trade forex, CFDs, futures and options, and invest in stocks, ETFs, bonds and funds, including through an ISA or SIPP.

Forex pricing is competitive for a full-service broker, with minimum spreads of 0.7 pips on EUR/USD and 0.8 on GBP/USD on the Classic tier, and 185+ currency pairs. Watch the small print: a fixed US$3 commission applies to forex trades under 50,000 units, so Saxo suits larger trades better than micro-lots. Prices improve if you qualify for the Platinum (£200,000+) or VIP (£1 million+) tiers.

Saxo's own platforms are its biggest strength. SaxoTrader is packed with professional tools, and TradingView can be linked for free. Saxo doesn't offer MetaTrader or spread betting, so traders who rely on Expert Advisors or want tax-free spread bets should look elsewhere.

Is Saxo safe?

  • Saxo Capital Markets UK Ltd is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 551422).
  • Client money is held in trust in segregated bank accounts, and client assets in segregated custodian accounts, separate from Saxo's own money.
  • Saxo files a monthly Client Money & Assets Return with the FCA, and its client money arrangements are audited every year by an independent auditor.
  • Eligible investments are protected by the FSCS up to £85,000 per person if Saxo were to fail.
  • Retail forex and CFD accounts have negative balance protection.

Saxo FAQs

Is Saxo safe?

Saxo Capital Markets UK Ltd is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 551422). Client money is held in trust in segregated accounts, audited annually, and eligible investments are covered by the FSCS up to £85,000.

What is Saxo's minimum deposit?

There is no minimum deposit to open a Saxo account. Higher tiers need £200,000 (Platinum) or £1 million (VIP).

What are Saxo's forex spreads?

On the Classic tier, Saxo's indicative minimum spreads are 0.7 pips on EUR/USD, 0.8 on GBP/USD and 0.9 on USD/JPY. A US$3 commission applies to trades under 50,000 units.

Does Saxo offer MetaTrader?

No. Saxo uses its own SaxoTrader and SaxoInvestor platforms, and supports TradingView and other third-party tools.

Does Saxo offer spread betting?

No. Saxo offers forex and CFDs for leveraged trading, but not spread betting.

Can I open an ISA or SIPP with Saxo?

Yes. Saxo UK offers a Stocks and Shares ISA and a SIPP.
