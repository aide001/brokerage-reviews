Our verdict on FP Markets

FP Markets was founded in Australia in 2005 and is regulated by ASIC in Australia, CySEC in Europe and the FSA in Seychelles, among others. It has no FCA-authorised company, so UK residents are not covered by UK protections such as the FSCS or the Financial Ombudsman Service.

Costs are very competitive. The Raw account has spreads from 0.0 pips plus US$3 per lot per side, and the Standard account has no commission with spreads from 1.0 pip. Both need only AUD 100 (or equivalent) to open, and FP Markets doesn't charge deposit fees or bank-wire withdrawal fees.

Platform choice is strong, with MT4, MT5, cTrader and TradingView, and FP Markets offers a large range of share CFDs on some platforms. For UK residents, though, the lack of FCA authorisation is the deciding factor. We suggest comparing FCA-authorised low-cost brokers such as Pepperstone, Tickmill or Vantage first.

Is FP Markets safe?

FP Markets FAQs

Is FP Markets regulated in the UK?

No. FP Markets is regulated by ASIC, CySEC and the Seychelles FSA, among others, but has no FCA-authorised company. UK residents don't get FSCS or Financial Ombudsman protection.

What is FP Markets' minimum deposit?

AUD 100 or the equivalent in your account currency, on both the Standard and Raw accounts.

What is FP Markets' commission?

The Raw account charges US$3 per lot per side (US$6 round turn). The Standard account has no commission.

Which platforms does FP Markets offer?

MT4, MT5, cTrader and TradingView, plus IRESS for share CFDs in some regions.

What are FCA-regulated alternatives to FP Markets?

Pepperstone, Tickmill and Vantage offer similar raw-spread pricing with FCA authorisation.
