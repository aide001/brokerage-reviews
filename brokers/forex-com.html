Our verdict on FOREX.com

FOREX.com is part of StoneX Group, a NASDAQ-listed financial services company with a history going back more than 100 years. In the UK, FOREX.com is now a trading name of StoneX Financial Ltd, which is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 446717). In September 2026 StoneX moved its UK retail business onto this single company, and the former UK entity, Gain Capital UK, applied to cancel its licence.

As the name suggests, FOREX.com is a currency specialist, with 80+ forex pairs and spreads on EUR/USD and GBP/USD from 0.7 points on the standard account. The minimum first deposit is £100, and card and bank deposits and standard withdrawals are free.

Platform choice is good: FOREX.com's own web platform and app, MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and TradingView. The main costs to watch are a £12 monthly inactivity fee after 12 months without trading, and a charge on same-day CHAPS withdrawals.

FOREX.com FAQs

Is FOREX.com safe?

In the UK, FOREX.com is a trading name of StoneX Financial Ltd, authorised by the FCA (FRN 446717). Retail client money is segregated, and the parent company StoneX Group is listed on NASDAQ.

What is FOREX.com's minimum deposit?

The minimum first deposit in the UK is £100.

Does FOREX.com charge an inactivity fee?

Yes. After 12 months with no trading, FOREX.com charges £12 a month until you trade again or the account has no funds.

What are FOREX.com's spreads?

On the UK standard account, EUR/USD and GBP/USD spreads start from 0.7 points, with no commission.

Does FOREX.com offer MT4, MT5 and TradingView?

Yes. FOREX.com offers its own platform plus MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and TradingView.

Why has FOREX.com's UK company changed?

In 2026 StoneX moved its UK retail forex and CFD business onto StoneX Financial Ltd. Gain Capital UK Limited, the previous UK entity, applied to cancel its FCA licence on 23 September 2026.
