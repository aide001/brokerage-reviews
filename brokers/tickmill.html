Our verdict on Tickmill

Tickmill UK Ltd is part of Tickmill Group and is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 717270). It is a lean, low-cost broker aimed squarely at active forex and CFD traders.

The Raw account is the main attraction: spreads from 0.0 pips plus a commission of US$3 per lot per side, one of the lowest commission rates among UK brokers. In Tickmill's own worked example, EUR/USD trades at a 0.2-pip spread. If you prefer no commission, the Classic account has spreads from 1.6 pips. Both accounts start from a 100 (GBP/USD/EUR) deposit, and Tickmill charges no deposit or withdrawal fees.

Tickmill is focused rather than broad. CFDs run on MT4, MT5 and TradingView, and there is no proprietary platform, spread betting or ISA. But Tickmill UK also offers futures and options, and an Interactive Brokers-powered multi-asset account, for traders who want more.

Is Tickmill safe?

  • Tickmill UK Ltd is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 717270).
  • Client funds are held in segregated bank accounts, separate from Tickmill's operating money, with regulated banking partners.
  • Tickmill UK is a member of the FSCS; Tickmill states eligible clients may be compensated up to £120,000 if it becomes insolvent.
  • Negative balance protection: if your account goes negative, Tickmill resets it to zero and takes the loss.

Tickmill FAQs

Is Tickmill safe?

Tickmill UK Ltd is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 717270). Client funds are segregated, retail clients have negative balance protection, and Tickmill UK is a member of the FSCS.

What is Tickmill's minimum deposit?

The starting deposit is 100 in your account currency (USD, EUR or GBP).

What is Tickmill's commission?

On the Raw account, Tickmill charges US$3 per lot per side (US$6 round turn) on forex and precious metals. The Classic account has no commission.

Does Tickmill offer TradingView?

Yes. Tickmill CFDs can be traded on MT4, MT5 and TradingView.

Does Tickmill offer spread betting?

No. Tickmill UK offers CFDs, futures and options, but not spread betting.

Does Tickmill charge withdrawal fees?

No. Tickmill doesn't charge for deposits or withdrawals; the minimum withdrawal is 25 in your account currency.
