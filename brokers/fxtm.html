Our verdict on FXTM

Important for UK readers: FXTM is no longer available to UK residents. Its former UK web pages now redirect to the global FXTM site, whose operating company, Exinity Limited (regulated by the Financial Services Commission of Mauritius), states that it does not provide services to residents of the United Kingdom.

Outside the UK, FXTM (ForexTime) is known for its strong following in emerging markets. It offers a range of MetaTrader accounts, from the Micro account (30 USD/EUR minimum) to the raw-spread Advantage account, which has spreads from 0 pips and a forex commission of US$3.50 per lot per side.

Because FXTM does not serve UK residents, we don't rank it in our UK guides. If you live in the UK, look at FCA-authorised alternatives with similar raw-spread pricing, such as Pepperstone, Tickmill or Vantage.

Is FXTM safe?

  • FXTM's global company, Exinity Limited, is regulated by the Financial Services Commission of Mauritius and licensed by South Africa's FSCA.
  • FXTM says it keeps client money separate from company capital with top-tier banks.
  • UK residents are not accepted, so FCA protections, including the FSCS, do not apply to FXTM's current offering.
  • Offshore leverage of up to 1:1000 greatly increases the risk of rapid losses.

FXTM FAQs

Can UK residents open an FXTM account?

No. FXTM's global company, Exinity Limited, states that it does not provide services to residents of the United Kingdom, and FXTM's UK pages now redirect to its global website.

Is FXTM regulated by the FCA?

FXTM's current offering is provided by Exinity Limited, regulated in Mauritius, not by the FCA. UK readers should choose an FCA-authorised broker.

What are good UK alternatives to FXTM?

For similar raw-spread pricing with FCA protection, consider Pepperstone, Tickmill or Vantage. See our Best Low Spread Brokers guide.

What is FXTM's Advantage account?

A raw-spread account with spreads from 0 pips and a forex commission of US$3.50 per lot per side, with a 500 USD/EUR/GBP minimum deposit (not available in the UK).
