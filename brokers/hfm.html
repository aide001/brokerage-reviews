Our verdict on HFM

HFM (formerly HotForex) is a global broker that serves UK clients through HF Markets (UK) Ltd, which is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 801701) and based in Holborn, London.

HFM's appeal is flexibility and low entry costs. The Premium account has no minimum deposit and no commission, with costs in the spread. The Zero account offers raw spreads plus a commission from US$3 per lot, with a US$25 minimum deposit. HFM doesn't charge deposit fees, and trading runs on MT4, MT5 and the HFM app.

The main thing to understand is that HFM's international website advertises features, such as leverage up to 1:2000, that are not available to UK retail clients. UK accounts follow FCA rules: leverage is capped at 1:30 on major forex pairs and you get negative balance protection. HFM's research and education are solid, but the product range and brand are less established in the UK than IG, CMC or Pepperstone.

Is HFM safe?

  • HF Markets (UK) Ltd is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 801701), registered office in London.
  • Client money is held in segregated bank accounts with highly rated banks, separate from HFM's own funds, and reconciled daily under FCA rules.
  • The FSCS can pay compensation of up to £85,000 per eligible client if HFM UK cannot meet its obligations.
  • Retail clients have negative balance protection and FCA leverage limits.

HFM FAQs

Is HFM regulated in the UK?

Yes. HF Markets (UK) Ltd is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 801701). Client money is segregated and eligible clients are covered by the FSCS up to £85,000.

What is HFM's minimum deposit?

The Premium account has no minimum deposit. The Zero account has a US$25 minimum.

Can UK clients get 1:2000 leverage at HFM?

No. High leverage is only offered by HFM's non-UK companies. UK retail clients are limited to 1:30 on major forex pairs under FCA rules.

What platforms does HFM offer?

MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and the HFM mobile app.

Does HFM charge deposit fees?

No. HFM does not charge deposit fees, although your bank or payment provider might.
