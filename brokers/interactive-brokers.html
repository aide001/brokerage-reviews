Our verdict on Interactive Brokers

Interactive Brokers (IBKR) is one of the largest brokers in the world. Its founder and chairman, Thomas Peterffy, started the business 49 years ago; the group now has equity capital of US$22.3 billion and more than 5 million client accounts. UK clients are served by Interactive Brokers (U.K.) Limited, which is authorised by the FCA (FRN 208159).

For forex, IBKR works differently from spread-betting brokers: you trade close to interbank prices and pay a separate commission of 0.20 basis points of the trade value (minimum US$2 per order) at the standard tier. For larger trades that is extremely cheap; for very small trades the US$2 minimum makes it less attractive. There is no minimum deposit and no inactivity fee.

Where IBKR really stands out is breadth: access to 170 market centres, 29 currencies, shares, ETFs, options, futures, bonds and funds, plus ISA accounts for UK investors. The flip side is complexity. Trader Workstation is powerful but has a steep learning curve, and IBKR does not offer spread betting.

Is Interactive Brokers safe?

  • Interactive Brokers (U.K.) Limited is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 208159).
  • Eligible UK clients are covered by the FSCS for up to £85,000 if the firm fails (FSCS does not cover investment losses).
  • The group reports equity capital of US$22.3 billion and US$13.9 billion of excess regulatory capital (Q2 2026 fact sheet).
  • Parent company Interactive Brokers Group is listed in the US and ranked in the Fortune 500 in 2025.
  • Retail CFD accounts have negative balance protection under FCA rules.

Interactive Brokers FAQs

Is Interactive Brokers safe?

Interactive Brokers (U.K.) Limited is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 208159), and eligible clients are covered by the FSCS up to £85,000. The group is one of the largest brokers in the world, with US$22.3 billion of equity capital.

What is the minimum deposit at Interactive Brokers?

There is no minimum deposit to open an IBKR account.

Does Interactive Brokers charge an inactivity fee?

No. IBKR does not charge inactivity or custody fees on standard accounts.

How much does forex cost at Interactive Brokers?

At the standard tier, forex costs 0.20 basis points of the trade value, with a US$2 minimum per order, on top of tight near-interbank prices.

Does Interactive Brokers offer spread betting?

No. IBKR UK offers forex, CFDs and a wide range of exchange-traded products, but not spread betting.

Can I open an ISA with Interactive Brokers?

Yes. IBKR UK offers a Stocks and Shares ISA, and SIPPs through approved SIPP administrators.

How many free withdrawals does Interactive Brokers allow?

Two free withdrawal requests per calendar month. After that, GBP withdrawals cost £1 by BACS or £7 by wire.
