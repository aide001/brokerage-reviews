Our verdict on Pepperstone

Pepperstone was founded in Melbourne in 2010 and serves UK clients through an FCA-authorised company (FRN 684312). It has become one of the most popular brokers for active traders, and says it has more than 900,000 accounts across its group.

Its big draw is price. On the Razor account, EUR/USD has averaged 0.1 pips (August 2026 data) with a fixed commission of £2.25 per lot per side for GBP accounts on MT4/MT5. If you prefer no commission, the Standard account and the spread betting account build costs into the spread instead. There are no account or inactivity fees, and deposits and withdrawals are free except international bank-wire withdrawals.

Platform choice is excellent: TradingView, MT4, MT5, cTrader and Pepperstone's own web platform and app. What you don't get is share dealing, ISAs or pensions. Pepperstone is a pure spread betting and CFD provider, so long-term investors will need a second account elsewhere.

Is Pepperstone safe?

  • Authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 684312) in the UK, and regulated in several other countries including by ASIC in Australia.
  • Client money is held in segregated accounts with top-tier banks, separate from Pepperstone's own money, and is not used for business operations or hedging.
  • If Pepperstone failed, eligible clients may be covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) for losses up to £85,000.
  • Retail accounts have negative balance protection, so you cannot lose more than your account balance.
  • Accounts are protected with encryption and two-factor authentication.

Pepperstone FAQs

Is Pepperstone safe?

Pepperstone's UK company is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 684312). Client money is kept in segregated accounts, and eligible clients are covered by the FSCS for up to £85,000 if the firm fails. Retail clients also get negative balance protection.

What is Pepperstone's minimum deposit?

There is no minimum by bank transfer. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay deposits start from £10.

What is the difference between Razor and Standard?

Razor has raw spreads from 0.0 pips plus a commission (£2.25 per lot per side on GBP MT4/MT5 accounts). Standard has no commission but wider spreads, from 1.0 pip on EUR/USD.

Does Pepperstone offer spread betting?

Yes. UK residents can open a spread betting account with no commission. Spread betting profits are currently free from UK Capital Gains Tax for most people, but tax rules depend on your circumstances.

Does Pepperstone charge an inactivity fee?

No. Pepperstone says it charges no account-keeping or inactivity fees, though very small, unused accounts may be archived.

Which platforms does Pepperstone offer?

TradingView, MT4, MT5, cTrader and its own Pepperstone web platform and mobile app.

How long do Pepperstone withdrawals take?

Pepperstone processes card, Apple Pay and Google Pay withdrawals within one working day and bank transfers within three working days. Your bank may take longer to credit the money.
