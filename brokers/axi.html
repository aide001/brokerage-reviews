Our verdict on Axi

Axi was founded in 2007 and says it now serves more than 1.4 million traders in over 100 countries. UK clients are served by an FCA-authorised company (FRN 509746).

Axi keeps things simple with two accounts. The Standard account has no commission and spreads from 0.68 pips on EUR/USD. The Pro account has spreads from 0 pips plus a US$4.50 round-trip commission. There is no required minimum deposit (Axi recommends US$100), and card, PayPal and bank deposits are free.

Platform choice has improved: as well as MetaTrader 4, UK clients can now use MetaTrader 5 and Axi's own trading platform, plus the Axi Copy Trading app. Research is lighter than at the big UK brokers, but Autochartist, free eBooks and the Axi Academy help newer traders.

Is Axi safe?

  • Axi's UK company is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 509746).
  • As an FCA-regulated firm, Axi must hold retail client money in segregated accounts under the FCA's client money rules.
  • Eligible clients may be covered by the FSCS if the UK firm fails (the investment limit is currently £85,000).
  • Retail accounts have negative balance protection and FCA leverage limits (30:1 on major forex pairs).
  • Axi's security page encourages clients to check contact details against the FCA register to avoid clone-firm scams.
