Our verdict on Exness

Important for UK readers: Exness does not serve UK residents, so you cannot open an account from the UK. We include this review because Exness is one of the largest retail brokers worldwide and UK readers often search for it.

Outside the UK, Exness is known for low entry costs and fast withdrawals, and says over 98% of withdrawals are processed automatically. The Standard account has no commission and, according to Exness, no minimum deposit. The professional accounts (Pro, Zero and Raw Spread) offer tighter pricing: Raw Spread starts from 0.0 pips with US$3.50 per lot per side, with minimum deposits of US$200 to US$3,000 depending on region.

Exness's international accounts are provided by offshore companies such as Exness (SC) Ltd, regulated in Seychelles, with extremely high leverage available. For UK residents, FCA-authorised brokers such as Pepperstone, IG or Tickmill are the relevant choices.

Is Exness safe?

  • Exness (SC) Ltd is regulated by the Financial Services Authority of Seychelles (licence SD025) and authorised in South Africa by the FSCA.
  • Exness (Cy) Ltd is regulated by CySEC in Cyprus (licence 178/12).
  • Exness says client funds are held segregated from its own funds.
  • Exness does not serve UK residents, so FCA protections, including the FSCS, do not apply.

Exness FAQs

Can UK residents trade with Exness?

No. Exness does not serve UK residents. UK traders should choose an FCA-authorised broker.

Is Exness regulated?

Yes, outside the UK: by the Seychelles FSA, CySEC and South Africa's FSCA, among others. It is not authorised by the FCA.

What is Exness's Raw Spread commission?

US$3.50 per lot per side, with spreads from 0.0 pips (not available in the UK).

What are good UK alternatives to Exness?

For low costs with FCA protection, consider Pepperstone, Tickmill or Vantage.
