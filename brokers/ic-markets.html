Our verdict on IC Markets

IC Markets was founded in Sydney in 2007 and is one of the world's biggest forex brokers by volume, popular with scalpers and algorithmic traders. Important for UK readers: IC Markets has no FCA-authorised company. The global website that UK residents reach is operated under a licence from the Financial Services Authority of Seychelles (FSA).

On price, IC Markets is excellent. The Raw Spread account has an average EUR/USD spread of 0.1 pips with a commission of US$3.50 per lot per side (US$7 round turn), and now has no minimum deposit. Newer Raw Pro accounts cut commission to US$1.50 or US$1.00 per side for larger deposits (US$5,000 and US$100,000).

The trade-off is protection. Without FCA authorisation, UK clients don't get FSCS cover, FCA leverage limits or the Financial Ombudsman Service. IC Markets offers leverage up to 1:5000 on MetaTrader, which can wipe out an account very quickly, though it does hold client-money insurance up to US$1 million. For UK residents, we suggest comparing FCA-authorised raw-spread brokers such as Pepperstone, Tickmill or Vantage first.

Is IC Markets safe?

  • IC Markets' global company is authorised and regulated by the Financial Services Authority of Seychelles (FSA). It is not authorised by the FCA.
  • UK clients of the offshore company are not covered by the FSCS or the Financial Ombudsman Service.
  • IC Markets holds client-money insurance covering funds up to US$1,000,000 (policy TT2602110), with no opt-in needed.
  • Client funds are kept in accounts separate from IC Markets' operating capital.
  • Leverage of up to 1:5000 is far above FCA limits; losses can exceed what you intended to risk very quickly.

IC Markets FAQs

Is IC Markets regulated in the UK?

No. IC Markets has no FCA-authorised company. UK residents deal with its Seychelles-regulated company, so FSCS and Financial Ombudsman protections do not apply.

What is IC Markets' minimum deposit?

The Raw Spread and Standard accounts now have no minimum deposit. Raw Pro needs US$5,000 and Raw Pro+ US$100,000.

What are IC Markets' spreads and commission?

On the Raw Spread account, EUR/USD averages 0.1 pips with US$3.50 per lot per side on MetaTrader. The Standard account has no commission and spreads from 0.8 pips.

Does IC Markets offer TradingView?

Yes, along with MT4, MT5 and cTrader.

What are FCA-regulated alternatives to IC Markets?

For similar raw-spread pricing with FCA protection, compare Pepperstone, Tickmill and Vantage in our Best Low Spread Brokers guide.
