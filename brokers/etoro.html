Our verdict on eToro

eToro, founded in 2007, is best known for social and copy trading: you can see what other investors are doing and automatically copy their portfolios. UK clients are served by eToro (UK) Ltd, which is authorised by the FCA (FRN 583263).

eToro prices CFDs as a percentage of the trade rather than in pips: EUR/USD costs 0.005% per trade, which works out at around 0.57 pips at today's exchange rate. UK clients can hold a GBP account, and withdrawals from it are free, but trading US assets from GBP or withdrawing from a USD account adds conversion costs or a US$5 fee. There are no account, management or inactivity fees, and no overnight or custody fees on unleveraged shares.

For beginners who want to invest and trade in one friendly app, including shares, ETFs, crypto and an ISA, eToro is hard to beat. Active forex traders will find it limited, though: there is no MetaTrader, no spread betting and far fewer advanced tools than at specialist brokers.

Is eToro safe?

  • eToro (UK) Ltd is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 583263).
  • Client funds are held in segregated accounts, separate from eToro's own money.
  • Money and assets held with eToro UK are treated as investments for the FSCS, with compensation of up to £85,000 per person if the firm fails.
  • Retail CFD accounts have negative balance protection.

eToro FAQs

Is eToro safe?

eToro (UK) Ltd is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 583263). Client money is kept in segregated accounts, and eligible UK clients are covered by the FSCS up to £85,000.

What is eToro's minimum deposit in the UK?

UK clients can make a first deposit from US$10, according to eToro's help centre.

Does eToro charge an inactivity fee?

No. eToro's fee page lists the inactivity fee as free.

What are eToro's forex spreads?

eToro charges 0.005% of the trade value on EUR/USD and GBP/USD, roughly 0.57 pips on EUR/USD at current rates.

Does eToro offer MT4 or spread betting?

No. eToro uses its own platform only and offers CFDs rather than spread bets.

Does eToro offer an ISA?

Yes. eToro offers a Stocks and Shares ISA and a Cash ISA to UK residents.

How much does it cost to withdraw from eToro?

Withdrawals from a GBP account are free. Withdrawals from a USD account cost US$5, with a US$30 minimum.
