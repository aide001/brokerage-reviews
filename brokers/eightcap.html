Our verdict on Eightcap

Eightcap Group Ltd is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 921296). The wider Eightcap group was founded in Melbourne in 2009 and is also regulated by ASIC in Australia and CySEC in Cyprus, with offshore entities in The Bahamas, Seychelles and Mauritius. UK clients are served by the FCA-regulated entity, with segregated client money, negative balance protection and FSCS cover.

For UK traders, Eightcap is a specialist TradingView broker: you connect your Eightcap account to TradingView and trade directly from its charts. That suits people who already do their analysis on TradingView, but UK clients don't get MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 or TradeLocker, which Eightcap offers through its non-UK entities, and there's no spread betting.

Costs are a strength. The Raw account offers spreads from 0.0 pips plus £2.25 per lot per side on GBP accounts (£4.50 round turn), which is towards the cheaper end for raw-spread pricing. The Standard account has no commission, with spreads from 1.0 pips. Research and education are thinner than at the largest brokers.

Is Eightcap safe?

  • Eightcap Group Ltd is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 921296), and is registered in England and Wales.
  • Client money is held in segregated accounts, separate from Eightcap's own funds, under the FCA's client money rules.
  • Eightcap Group Ltd is covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS); eligible retail clients may be able to claim up to £85,000 if the firm fails.
  • Negative balance protection applies to retail clients, so you can't lose more than the money in your account.
  • Beware of clones: the FCA has warned about a fake firm impersonating Eightcap. Only use eightcap.com and check FRN 921296 on the FCA register.

Eightcap FAQs

Is Eightcap regulated in the UK?

Yes. Eightcap Group Ltd is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 921296) and is covered by the FSCS.

What is Eightcap's minimum deposit?

US$100, or the equivalent in your account currency, for both the Standard and Raw accounts.

What commission does Eightcap charge?

On the Raw account, £2.25 per lot per side on GBP accounts (£4.50 round turn), or US$3.50 per side on USD accounts. The Standard account has no commission; costs are built into spreads from 1.0 pips.

Can UK clients use MetaTrader with Eightcap?

No. UK clients of Eightcap Group Ltd trade through TradingView. MetaTrader 4 and 5 are offered by Eightcap's non-UK entities.

Does Eightcap offer spread betting?

No. Eightcap offers CFDs only to UK clients, so there's no spread betting account.

Can I trade crypto with Eightcap in the UK?

Not as a retail client. The FCA bans the sale of crypto CFDs to UK retail customers.
