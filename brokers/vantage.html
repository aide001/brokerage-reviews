Our verdict on Vantage

Vantage serves UK clients through Vantage Global Prime LLP, which is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 590299). The group is celebrating 15 years in business.

Vantage's big selling point is price. On the RAW ECN account, UK clients with a GBP account pay just £1 per lot per side (£2 round turn) on top of raw spreads from 0.0 pips, among the lowest commissions of any FCA-authorised broker. Larger traders can cut that to £0.75 per side on the Premium ECN account (£25,000 minimum). The minimum deposit on the Standard STP and RAW ECN accounts is just 50 in your account currency.

Vantage also stands out on protection: on top of FSCS cover, it has Lloyd's of London 'excess of FSCS' insurance covering client funds up to £1 million. The trade-offs are a smaller research offering than the big UK names, and no share dealing or ISA.

Is Vantage safe?

  • Vantage Global Prime LLP is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 590299).
  • Client funds are kept in segregated trust accounts with NatWest, so they are identifiable as client money and protected from Vantage's creditors.
  • Funds lost in a liquidation can be compensated up to £85,000 under the FSCS.
  • Vantage also has 'excess of FSCS' insurance with Lloyd's of London, covering client funds up to £1,000,000 in total.
  • Retail accounts have negative balance protection under FCA rules.

Vantage FAQs

Is Vantage safe for UK traders?

Vantage Global Prime LLP is authorised by the FCA (FRN 590299). Client money is held in segregated trust accounts at NatWest, covered by the FSCS up to £85,000, with additional Lloyd's of London insurance up to £1 million.

What is Vantage's minimum deposit?

The Standard STP and RAW ECN accounts need a minimum of 50 in your account currency. The Premium ECN account needs 25,000.

What is Vantage's commission?

On the RAW ECN account, GBP accounts pay £1 per lot per side (£2 round turn). Premium ECN is £0.75 per side. The Standard STP account has no commission.

Does Vantage offer spread betting?

Yes. Vantage UK offers a spread betting account alongside its CFD accounts.

Does Vantage support TradingView?

Yes. Vantage supports TradingView, MT4, MT5, its own app and the ProTrader web platform.
