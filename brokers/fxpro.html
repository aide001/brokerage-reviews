Our verdict on FxPro

FxPro UK Limited is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 509956) and has been since 2010. The wider FxPro group was founded in 2006 and is also licensed by CySEC in Cyprus, the FSCA in South Africa and the Securities Commission of The Bahamas. FxPro says it has more than 11 million client accounts across 173 countries.

FxPro's biggest strength is choice. You can trade on MT4, MT5, cTrader, TradingView (via a cTrader account) or FxPro's own platform, and UK residents can also spread bet on the FxPro Edge platform. It offers more than 2,100 instruments across forex, shares, indices, futures, metals, energy and crypto CFDs.

On costs, FxPro sits in the middle of the pack. The Raw+ and cTrader accounts offer forex spreads from 0 plus a commission of US$3.50 per lot per side. The Standard account has no commission but spreads from 1.2 pips. FxPro doesn't charge for deposits or withdrawals and has no fixed minimum deposit, though it recommends starting with US$1,000.

Is FxPro safe?

  • FxPro UK Limited is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 509956).
  • Client funds are fully segregated from FxPro's own money and held in separate bank accounts.
  • FxPro UK Limited is a member of the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS); eligibility depends on your status and the nature of the claim.
  • Negative balance protection applies to all clients, so you can't lose more than you deposit.
  • Beware of clones: the FCA has published a warning about a fake firm using the name 'Fx Pro'. Always check you are on FxPro's genuine website.

FxPro FAQs

Is FxPro regulated in the UK?

Yes. FxPro UK Limited is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 509956) and has been since 2010. It is also a member of the FSCS.

What is FxPro's minimum deposit?

FxPro has no fixed minimum deposit, but it recommends an initial deposit of US$1,000.

What commission does FxPro charge?

On Raw+ accounts, US$3.50 per lot per side (US$7 round turn) on forex and metals. On cTrader, US$35 per US$1 million traded. The Standard account has no commission; costs are built into spreads from 1.2 pips.

Does FxPro offer spread betting?

Yes. Clients of FxPro UK Limited who live in the UK can spread bet on the FxPro Edge platform.

Does FxPro work with TradingView?

Yes. You can connect an FxPro cTrader account to TradingView and place trades directly from its charts.

Does FxPro charge deposit or withdrawal fees?

No. FxPro doesn't charge for deposits or withdrawals, although your bank or payment provider might.
