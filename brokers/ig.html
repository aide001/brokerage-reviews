Our verdict on IG

IG is the UK's longest-running spread betting and CFD provider. It was founded in 1974, is authorised by the FCA, and its parent company, IG Group, is listed on the London Stock Exchange. For UK traders that track record, combined with segregated client money and FSCS cover, is hard to beat.

Costs are competitive rather than rock-bottom. EUR/USD starts from 0.6 pips with no commission, there are no account, platform or inactivity fees, and deposits and withdrawals are free. Overnight funding costs more on spread bets than on CFDs, so check which account suits how long you hold trades.

Where IG really stands out is choice. One login gives you spread betting, CFDs, share dealing, ISAs and a SIPP, and you can trade on IG's own platform, MT4, MT5, ProRealTime, L2 Dealer or TradingView. That breadth can feel busy for a complete beginner, but the education, daily IG TV shows and 24-hour support go a long way to help.

Is IG safe?

  • Authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 195355 for IG Markets Ltd).
  • Client money is held in segregated trust accounts at high-street banks such as Barclays and Lloyds, never mixed with IG's own money, and not used for IG's hedging.
  • Shares you buy are held by approved custodians in segregated nominee accounts under the FCA's client asset (CASS) rules.
  • If IG failed, any shortfall of up to £85,000 may be covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).
  • Retail accounts have negative balance protection, so you cannot lose more than the money in your account.
  • Parent company IG Group is listed on the London Stock Exchange and publishes audited accounts.

Research and education

IG's in-house IG TV channel runs live shows three times a day, Monday to Friday: a pre-European open bulletin at 7:30am, 'Charting the markets' at 10:30am and a trading look-ahead at 4pm (UK time). You also get news and analysis built into the platform, trading signals, an economic calendar, a stock screener and the IG Academy for structured lessons.

Customer support

Support is available 24 hours a day except from 10pm Friday to 8am Saturday (UK time), through web chat and WhatsApp. There is also a UK freephone number, 0800 195 3100, and a detailed online help centre.

IG FAQs

Is IG safe?

IG is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 195355), has been in business since 1974 and its parent company is listed on the London Stock Exchange. Client money is kept in segregated trust accounts, and eligible clients are covered by the FSCS for up to £85,000 if IG were to fail. As with any leveraged product, you can still lose the money you trade with.

What is IG's minimum deposit?

There is no minimum deposit by bank transfer. IG's help centre lists a £1 minimum for card deposits. You will still need enough funds to cover the margin on any trade you open.

Does IG charge an inactivity fee?

No. IG's charges page lists account opening, closing and maintenance, and inactivity, as free.

What are IG's spreads?

IG's published minimum spreads include 0.6 pips on EUR/USD, 0.9 on GBP/USD, 1 point on the FTSE 100 and 0.4 on the S&P 500. Spreads are variable and can widen outside market hours and during volatile periods.

Does IG offer MT4, MT5 and TradingView?

Yes. As well as its own web platform and apps, IG offers MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, ProRealTime, L2 Dealer and TradingView integration.

Is spread betting with IG tax-free?

For most UK residents, spread betting profits are currently free from Capital Gains Tax and Stamp Duty. Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and can change, so check with HMRC or a tax adviser if you are unsure.

What is IG's minimum withdrawal?

IG's help centre lists a £100 minimum for withdrawals by card, Apple Pay or bank transfer. Card and Apple Pay withdrawals are limited to £20,000 a day; bank transfers have no maximum.

Does IG have a demo account?

Yes. You can open a free demo account to practise on IG's platform with virtual funds.
