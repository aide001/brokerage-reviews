Our verdict on AvaTrade

Important for UK readers: AvaTrade's help centre states that it does not accept clients from the United Kingdom. We include this review because AvaTrade is a well-known global broker that UK readers often search for.

Outside the UK, AvaTrade is regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland (AvaTrade EU Ltd), ASIC in Australia and South Africa's FSCA, among others. It is known for spread-only pricing, the AvaProtect risk-management feature and a wide platform choice including MT4, MT5 and its AvaTradeGO app.

Watch the non-trading fees: AvaTrade charges an inactivity fee of £50/$50/€50 after three consecutive months without use, and an administration fee of £100/$100/€100 after 12 months. For UK residents, FCA-authorised brokers such as IG, CMC Markets or Pepperstone are the relevant alternatives.

Is AvaTrade safe?

  • AvaTrade EU Ltd is regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland; other AvaTrade companies are licensed by ASIC (Australia, licence 406684) and the FSCA (South Africa, FSP 45984), among others.
  • AvaTrade does not accept UK clients, so FCA protections, including the FSCS, do not apply.
  • Retail clients of EU-regulated AvaTrade companies get negative balance protection under EU rules.
