Our verdict on OANDA

OANDA has more than 25 years of experience and is best known as a forex specialist. UK clients are served by OANDA Europe Limited, which is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 542574).

Pricing is straightforward: spreads start from 0.6 pips on EUR/USD, 1 point on the UK 100 and 1.2 points on the Germany 40, with no commission on the standard account. There is no minimum deposit, and OANDA charges no deposit or withdrawal fees. There is an inactivity fee (up to £20 a month) once an account has had no trades for 12 months.

OANDA has broadened its offer in recent years. A standard account now includes a spread betting sub-account, and you can trade on OANDA's own web and mobile platforms, MT4, MT5 or TradingView. The market range is still narrower than at IG or CMC, so OANDA suits forex-focused traders best.

Is OANDA safe?

  • OANDA Europe Limited is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 542574) and follows the FCA's client money (CASS) rules.
  • Client money is ring-fenced in segregated bank accounts held under trust, and cannot be used for OANDA's business activities.
  • Retail clients have negative balance protection, so losses are limited to the funds in your account.
  • Eligible clients may be able to claim from the FSCS if OANDA Europe Limited fails (the FSCS investment limit is currently £85,000).
  • The OANDA group is also regulated in the US, Canada, Australia, Singapore and other countries.

OANDA FAQs

Is OANDA safe?

OANDA Europe Limited is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 542574). Client money is held in segregated trust accounts, and retail clients have negative balance protection.

What is OANDA's minimum deposit?

There is no minimum deposit or minimum balance to open or keep an OANDA account.

Does OANDA charge an inactivity fee?

Yes. After 12 months with no trading, OANDA charges up to £20 a month per client. No fee is taken while you have an open position, and you can ask for up to three months back if you start trading again.

Does OANDA offer spread betting?

Yes. UK clients can open a spread betting sub-account within the standard account.

Which platforms does OANDA offer?

OANDA's own web platform and mobile app, MT4, MT5 and TradingView, plus an API.

What are OANDA's spreads?

OANDA's spreads start from 0.6 pips on EUR/USD, 1.3 pips on USD/JPY, 1 point on the UK 100 and 1.2 points on the Germany 40.
