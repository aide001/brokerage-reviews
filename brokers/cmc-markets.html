Our verdict on CMC Markets

CMC Markets has been around since 1989 and has offered spread betting online since 2001. Its UK company, CMC Markets UK plc, is authorised by the FCA (FRN 173730), and the group is a member of the FTSE 250. For UK traders it is one of the most established names in the market.

Its strongest point is range. CMC offers more than 21,000 instruments for spread betting and CFDs, including around 300 forex pairs, and adds share dealing, a Stocks and Shares ISA and a SIPP for investing. There is no minimum deposit, spread bets have no commission, and UK and European share CFDs are commission-free.

The trade-offs are a £10 monthly inactivity fee after a year with no trades, and a feature-packed platform that takes time to learn. For active traders who want advanced charting, pattern recognition and a very wide choice of markets, CMC is one of the best-equipped brokers in the UK.

Is CMC Markets safe?

  • CMC Markets UK plc is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 173730) and must follow the FCA's client asset (CASS) rules.
  • Retail client money is held in segregated accounts spread across several major banks, and is not used as margin with CMC's hedging counterparties.
  • Eligible clients are protected by the FSCS for up to £85,000 if CMC were to fail.
  • Retail accounts have negative balance protection.
  • CMC Markets is a FTSE 250 company that has been in business since 1989.

CMC Markets FAQs

Is CMC Markets safe?

CMC Markets UK plc is authorised and regulated by the FCA (FRN 173730). Retail client money is segregated across several major banks, and eligible clients are protected by the FSCS for up to £85,000. The group is a FTSE 250 company founded in 1989.

What is CMC Markets' minimum deposit?

There is no minimum deposit to open an account. You will need enough money to cover the margin on the trades you place.

Does CMC Markets charge an inactivity fee?

Yes. After a year with no trading, CMC charges £10 a month on dormant accounts. Nothing is deducted if the account has no funds.

What are CMC Markets' spreads?

CMC advertises spreads from 0.5 pips on major forex pairs, 1 point on the US NDAQ 100 and 1.2 points on the Germany 40. The FX Active account offers 0.0 pips on six major pairs plus a 0.0025% commission.

Does CMC Markets offer MT4 and TradingView?

Yes. As well as its own web platform and app, CMC offers MetaTrader 4, and CFD accounts can be linked to TradingView.

Can I open an ISA with CMC Markets?

Yes. CMC offers a Stocks and Shares ISA, a general investment account and a SIPP, with commission-free share dealing.

How long do CMC Markets withdrawals take?

UK bank withdrawals usually take one to two working days, international bank withdrawals three to five working days, and card withdrawals from an hour to five working days depending on your card issuer.
